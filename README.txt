SOUNDWAVE MUSIC WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: website layout and content
- style.css: design, colors, responsive layout
- script.js: search, local audio player controls, playlist interactions

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Keep index.html, style.css and script.js in the same folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in a modern browser.

MUSIC PLAYER
- Click “Add your music” and choose audio files from your device.
- The player supports local audio files selected by the visitor.
- Play/pause, seek, volume, previous/next, shuffle and repeat controls are included.
- Sample track names, artists, albums and playlists are illustrative UI content only.
  No copyrighted songs or audio files are included.

NOTES
- The page uses Google Fonts loaded online; an internet connection is needed for those fonts.
- This is a front-end demo. It does not stream from a music catalogue, save accounts,
  or store playlists between visits. A real streaming service needs licensed audio,
  a backend, user accounts and appropriate music rights.
